Dekomposisi QR dan QR Iteration#

Matriks A#

Diberikan matriks:

\[\begin{split} A= \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 1\\ 1 & 2 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]
from sympy import Matrix

A = Matrix([
    [2, 1],
    [1, 2]
])

print(A)

Output:

Matrix([[2, 1], [1, 2]])

Tahap 1 — Membentuk Vektor q₁#

Kolom pertama matriks A:

\[\begin{split} a_1= \begin{bmatrix} 2\\ 1 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

Menghitung Panjang Vektor a₁#

Rumus norma vektor:

\[ \|a_1\| = \sqrt{2^2+1^2} = \sqrt{5} \]
from sympy import Matrix, sqrt

a1 = Matrix([
    [2],
    [1]
])

norma_a1 = sqrt(2**2 + 1**2)

print("Norma a1 =", norma_a1)

Output:

Norma a1 = sqrt(5)

Menghitung q₁#

Rumus:

\[ q_1=\frac{a_1}{\|a_1\|} \]

Maka:

\[\begin{split} q_1= \frac{1}{\sqrt5} \begin{bmatrix} 2\\ 1 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} \dfrac{2}{\sqrt5}\\ \dfrac{1}{\sqrt5} \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]
from sympy import Matrix, sqrt

a1 = Matrix([
    [2],
    [1]
])

q1 = a1 / sqrt(5)

print(q1)

Output:

Matrix([
[2/sqrt(5)],
[1/sqrt(5)]
])

Tahap 2 — Menghitung Dot Product q₁ · a₂#

Kolom kedua matriks A:

\[\begin{split} a_2= \begin{bmatrix} 1\\ 2 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

Rumus dot product:

\[ q_1 \cdot a_2 = \left(\frac{2}{\sqrt5}\times1\right) + \left(\frac{1}{\sqrt5}\times2\right) \]

Hasil:

\[ q_1 \cdot a_2 = \frac{4}{\sqrt5} \]
from sympy import Matrix, sqrt

a1 = Matrix([
    [2],
    [1]
])

a2 = Matrix([
    [1],
    [2]
])

q1 = a1 / sqrt(5)

dot = q1.dot(a2)

print(dot)

Output:

4/sqrt(5)

Tahap 3 — Menghitung v₂#

Rumus Gram-Schmidt:

\[ v_2 = a_2-(q_1\cdot a_2)q_1 \]

Substitusi:

\[\begin{split} v_2 = \begin{bmatrix} 1\\ 2 \end{bmatrix} - \frac{4}{\sqrt5} \begin{bmatrix} \dfrac{2}{\sqrt5}\\ \dfrac{1}{\sqrt5} \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

Hasil proyeksi:

\[\begin{split} (q_1\cdot a_2)q_1 = \begin{bmatrix} \dfrac85\\ \dfrac45 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

Maka:

\[\begin{split} v_2 = \begin{bmatrix} 1\\ 2 \end{bmatrix} - \begin{bmatrix} \dfrac85\\ \dfrac45 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} -\dfrac35\\ \dfrac65 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]
from sympy import Matrix, sqrt

a1 = Matrix([
    [2],
    [1]
])

a2 = Matrix([
    [1],
    [2]
])

q1 = a1 / sqrt(5)

proj = q1.dot(a2) * q1

v2 = a2 - proj

print(v2)

Output:

Matrix([
[-3/5],
[ 6/5]
])

Tahap 4 — Normalisasi v₂ Menjadi q₂#

Norma v₂:

\[ \|v_2\| = \sqrt{ \left(-\frac35\right)^2 + \left(\frac65\right)^2 } \]
\[ = \sqrt{ \frac9{25} + \frac{36}{25} } \]
\[ = \sqrt{\frac{45}{25}} = \frac3{\sqrt5} \]

Menghitung q₂#

Rumus:

\[ q_2 = \frac{v_2}{\|v_2\|} \]

Maka:

\[\begin{split} q_2 = \begin{bmatrix} -\dfrac1{\sqrt5}\\ \dfrac2{\sqrt5} \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]
from sympy import Matrix, sqrt

v2 = Matrix([
    [-3/5],
    [ 6/5]
])

norma_v2 = sqrt(v2.dot(v2))

q2 = v2 / norma_v2

print(q2)

Output:

Matrix([
[-1/sqrt(5)],
[ 2/sqrt(5)]
])

Tahap 5 — Membentuk Matriks Q#

Matriks Q dibentuk dari q₁ dan q₂:

\[\begin{split} Q= \begin{bmatrix} \dfrac2{\sqrt5} & -\dfrac1{\sqrt5}\\ \dfrac1{\sqrt5} & \dfrac2{\sqrt5} \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]
from sympy import Matrix, sqrt

q1 = Matrix([
    [2/sqrt(5)],
    [1/sqrt(5)]
])

q2 = Matrix([
    [-1/sqrt(5)],
    [ 2/sqrt(5)]
])

Q = Matrix.hstack(q1, q2)

print(Q)

Output:

Matrix([
[2/sqrt(5), -1/sqrt(5)],
[1/sqrt(5),  2/sqrt(5)]
])

Tahap 6 — Menghitung Matriks R#

Rumus:

\[ R=Q^TA \]

Hasil:

\[\begin{split} R= \begin{bmatrix} \sqrt5 & \dfrac4{\sqrt5}\\ 0 & \dfrac3{\sqrt5} \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]
from sympy import Matrix, sqrt

A = Matrix([
    [2, 1],
    [1, 2]
])

Q = Matrix([
    [2/sqrt(5), -1/sqrt(5)],
    [1/sqrt(5),  2/sqrt(5)]
])

R = Q.T * A

print(R)

Output:

Matrix([
[sqrt(5), 4/sqrt(5)],
[      0, 3/sqrt(5)]
])

Tahap 7 — Verifikasi QR = A#

Kalikan:

\[ QR \]
\[\begin{split} = \begin{bmatrix} \dfrac2{\sqrt5} & -\dfrac1{\sqrt5}\\ \dfrac1{\sqrt5} & \dfrac2{\sqrt5} \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} \sqrt5 & \dfrac4{\sqrt5}\\ 0 & \dfrac3{\sqrt5} \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

Hasil:

\[\begin{split} QR= \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 1\\ 1 & 2 \end{bmatrix} =A \end{split}\]
from sympy import Matrix, sqrt

Q = Matrix([
    [2/sqrt(5), -1/sqrt(5)],
    [1/sqrt(5),  2/sqrt(5)]
])

R = Matrix([
    [sqrt(5), 4/sqrt(5)],
    [0,       3/sqrt(5)]
])

print(Q * R)

Output:

Matrix([
[2, 1],
[1, 2]
])

Tahap 8 — Iterasi QR Pertama#

Rumus iterasi:

\[ A_{k+1}=R_kQ_k \]

Iterasi pertama:

\[ A_1 = RQ \]
\[\begin{split} = \begin{bmatrix} 2.8 & 0.6\\ 0.6 & 1.2 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]
from sympy import Matrix, sqrt

Q = Matrix([
    [2/sqrt(5), -1/sqrt(5)],
    [1/sqrt(5),  2/sqrt(5)]
])

R = Matrix([
    [sqrt(5), 4/sqrt(5)],
    [0,       3/sqrt(5)]
])

A1 = R * Q

print(A1)

Output:

Matrix([
[14/5, 3/5],
[ 3/5, 6/5]
])

Tahap 9 — QR Iteration 10 Kali#

from sympy import Matrix, sqrt, N, pprint

A = Matrix([
    [2, 1],
    [1, 2]
])

print("A0 =")
pprint(A)

for k in range(1, 11):

    a1 = A[:, 0]
    a2 = A[:, 1]

    # Gram-Schmidt
    q1 = a1 / sqrt(a1.dot(a1))

    proj = q1.dot(a2) * q1

    v2 = a2 - proj

    q2 = v2 / sqrt(v2.dot(v2))

    # Susun Q dan R
    Q = Matrix.hstack(q1, q2)

    R = Q.T * A

    # Update matriks
    A = R * Q

    print(f"\nA{k} =")
    pprint(N(A, 6))

Hasil Iterasi#

\[\begin{split} A_1= \begin{bmatrix} 2.8 & 0.6\\ 0.6 & 1.2 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]
\[\begin{split} A_2= \begin{bmatrix} 2.96 & 0.28\\ 0.28 & 1.04 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]
\[\begin{split} A_3= \begin{bmatrix} 2.9918 & 0.1233\\ 0.1233 & 1.0082 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]
\[\begin{split} A_4= \begin{bmatrix} 2.9986 & 0.0549\\ 0.0549 & 1.0014 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]
\[\begin{split} A_5= \begin{bmatrix} 2.9997 & 0.0244\\ 0.0244 & 1.0003 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]
\[\begin{split} A_{10} \approx \begin{bmatrix} 3 & 0\\ 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

Kesimpulan#

Metode QR Iteration berhasil menemukan nilai eigen matriks:

\[\begin{split} A= \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 1\\ 1 & 2 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

Nilai diagonal matriks hasil iterasi mendekati:

\[ \lambda_1=3 \]
\[ \lambda_2=1 \]

Jadi nilai eigen matriks A adalah:

\[ \lambda_1=3 \]
\[ \lambda_2=1 \]
